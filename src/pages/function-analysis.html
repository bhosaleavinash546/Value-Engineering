Function analysis and FAST diagrams

What separates Value Engineering from a discount hunt: express the product as verb–noun functions, map their logic in a FAST diagram, load every pound of cost onto the functions it serves — then attack the mismatches with arithmetic, not anecdotes. Worked live on an everyday product: the electric kettle.

Function–cost matrix & value index

Allocate the kettle's £12.40 works cost to its functions, estimate each function's worth — the lowest cost that could still achieve it — and the mismatches surface themselves. VI = Cost ÷ Worth; anything above 2.0 is a creative-phase target.
