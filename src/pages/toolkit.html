Worksheets you can use in your own studies. Most come as a page you can print or save as a PDF, and as an Excel file with the formulas already set up, which you can extend for as many parts as you need. The board pack is a PowerPoint you fill in. They’re free to use yourself or inside your company, as long as you credit VAVEhub. Please don’t resell or republish them.
