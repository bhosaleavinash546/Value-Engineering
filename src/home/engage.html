About the author

I take cost out of products,
not value.

I'm a value engineer with fourteen years of hands-on work in automotive propulsion and chassis, construction equipment and engineering services. Day to day that means competitor teardowns, should-cost models and VA/VE workshops with mixed teams, and lately AI tools that make cost analysis much faster.

VAVEhub is where I share the method properly: the guides, the templates and two courses, all free.

Most cost problems are really value problems. The aim is to get more of what a product does for every pound it costs, without making it cheap.
