Industry ranges are typical yearly savings for an established programme; a first study on a product nobody has studied usually finds 8–15%. A typical study returns more than 10 times its cost and pays back in under 12 months. Two to four weeks of groundwork turns this range into a checked figure for each part.

About Avinash Bhosale

Creator of VAVEhub · Senior Cost Improvement Engineer

VAVEhub is a free site built on fourteen years of hands-on value engineering in automotive propulsion and chassis, construction equipment and engineering services. The work covers competitor benchmarking, should-cost modelling, running VA/VE workshops, and AI tools for cost analysis. Everything here, from the guides and templates to the Academy, is shared to help engineers and buyers learn the method properly.

"Most cost problems are really value problems. The aim is to get more of what a product does for every pound it costs, without making it cheap."

VAVEhub is a personal, non-commercial project. It isn't connected with or endorsed by any employer, past or present, and the views here are my own.
