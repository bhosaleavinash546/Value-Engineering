Questions people ask

Who is it for?

Leaders and managers who set budgets, targets and product plans: engineering, purchasing, operations, finance and general management. You don't need a technical background.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

It's free. You can read or listen to every module without an account. Sign in only if you want to save your progress or get the certificate.

What is the certificate?

A VAVE for Leaders certificate of completion, for passing the 12-question quiz with 70% or more. Anyone can check it online. It's an overview course, not a professional certification.

Can I use it with my team?

Yes. Play a module in a meeting, use the readiness check and the pilot picker together, and download the executive pack (business case, sponsor charter and board slides) from Module 6.

What comes next?

The full VE Academy: 13 modules, hands-on labs, a capstone study and a 30-question exam. It's free too.
