Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. All 13 modules, the labs, the templates, the exam and the certificate are free, and there's no paid upgrade. After Module 1 you need a free account, so your progress and your certificate are saved.

What is the certificate, and what isn't it?

It's a VAVEhub Certificate of Completion. It shows you finished the course and passed its 30-question exam with 80% or more, and anyone can check it at valueengineeringhub.com/verify. It isn't a professional certification such as SAVE International's VMA or CVS, and no certification body accredits the course.

How long does it take?

About six hours in all. Each module takes 15 to 40 minutes, and you can stop and pick up where you left off, on any device once you're signed in.

Do I have to take the modules in order?

Each module builds on the one before, so in order works best, but you can open any module once you're signed in. The exam takes two questions from every module.

Can my company use the templates?

Yes. The templates in the toolkit are free to use yourself or inside your company, as long as you credit VAVEhub.
