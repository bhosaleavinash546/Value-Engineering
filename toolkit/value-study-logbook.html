Value Study Logbook

One line for every value study you take part in, signed while people still remember it. Later certification applications ask for this kind of evidence.

v1.0 · Updated Oct 2026Keep it from your first study

Professional bodies ask for real study experience. For example, the Institute of Value Management's QVA asks for evidence of taking part in two value studies, and its PVM asks you to have led at least two. SAVE International's CVS asks for study experience too. Check each body's current rules before you apply. This log just keeps the facts in one place.
