What to expect (Module 12): a first proper round on a product nobody has studied usually finds 8–15% of product cost; an established programme keeps finding 3–5% a year. Worked example, year 2: funnel coverage 64%, cost visibility 83%, 41% of approved ideas implemented, £1.9M of checked savings a quarter, average payback 8.2 months.
