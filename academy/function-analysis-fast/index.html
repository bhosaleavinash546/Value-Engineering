From the module

What is a function?

Look at the pen on your desk. Most people see a thing: a tube, a spring, a clip, some ink. A value engineer sees jobs: the ink makes marks, the spring stores energy, the clip secures the pen to a pocket. A function is the job a thing does, not the thing itself.

Customers pay for jobs done. Nobody wants a heating element; they want hot water. Nobody wants six screws; they want two parts held together. Describe the product as jobs and you can ask: "what else could do this job, better or for less?"
