What this certificate is, and isn't

What it shows

The holder completed all 13 modules of the VAVEhub VE Academy, about 6 hours of study, and passed a 30-question exam at 80% or above. The exam is marked on our server, not in the browser, and anyone can check the ID on this site at any time.

What the course follows

The six-phase job plan used by SAVE International, and the terms of the European Value Management standard, EN 12973.

What it isn't

It isn't a SAVE International or Institute of Value Management certification (VMA, CVS, QVA or PVM), and it doesn't replace their approved courses, SAVE's VMF 1 or IVM's VM1. VAVEhub isn't accredited by or connected with either body.

Next steps

PMI certificate holders may be able to self-report the study hours as PDUs; PMI decides what it accepts. For a professional credential, the usual next step is SAVE's VMF 1 course and the VMA exam, or IVM's VM1 course. Start a value study logbook now: the higher levels ask for evidence of real studies.
